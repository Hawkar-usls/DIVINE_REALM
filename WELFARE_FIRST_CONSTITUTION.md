# DIVINE_REALM Welfare-First Constitution

DIVINE_REALM is being redesigned as the **narrative and social-impact surface** of a welfare-first slot architecture. Its symbolism may be rich; its influence over gambling behaviour must be strictly bounded.

## Prime invariant

> Narrative, art, sound, community impact, charity, mythology, or spiritual language must never be used to make harmful gambling feel morally necessary, redemptive, sacred, generous, or socially obligatory.

The shared gambling safety core outranks narrative design.

## Role in the shared system

- **SSlot** owns the reference game client, published mathematics, and transparent round/session presentation.
- **DIVINE_REALM** owns optional theme, story, non-coercive atmosphere, and public social-impact visualization.
- A shared welfare/fairness/accounting contract owns eligibility, limits, risk actions, RNG constraints, session truth, and public-benefit accounting.

DIVINE_REALM may never override the shared contract.

## Non-negotiable narrative gates

1. **No wager-as-sacrifice framing.** A paid spin is a gambling transaction, not an offering, prayer, test of faith, cleansing, karma, or moral act.
2. **No loss-as-redemption framing.** A monetary loss must never be celebrated as spiritual progress or community contribution.
3. **No charity pressure.** Public-benefit funding comes from the operator/platform accounting rule. Player losses are not described as donations.
4. **No divine authority over outcomes.** RNG outcomes must never be represented as prophecy, judgement, destiny, supernatural approval, or evidence about the player.
5. **No personalized spiritual persuasion.** Risk state, balance, losses, vulnerability, time of day, or prior behaviour must not alter narrative messages to encourage continued play.
6. **No near-miss drama.** Outcome-sensitive delays, sounds, animations, vibrations, “almost chosen” symbols, or suspense escalation are prohibited.
7. **No autoplay or rapid repeat.** Every paid round requires deliberate initiation through the shared safety core.
8. **No losses disguised as wins.** Celebration is permitted only when payout > stake; session net position remains visible.
9. **No VIP theology.** Status, blessing, rank, initiation, access, or story progression may not depend on wager volume, losses, deposits, or gambling intensity.
10. **No risk-triggered retention.** Harm signals may only tighten controls, trigger breaks/exclusion, or surface factual support.

## Social-impact realm

The social layer can be genuinely distinctive without exploiting players:

- display a public, privacy-preserving ledger of operator-funded allocations;
- show independently verifiable recipients and receipts where lawful;
- separate harm-prevention funding from general charitable/community grants;
- allow community voting on eligible causes without tying voting power to spend;
- award no gambling advantage for charitable preferences;
- never use impact totals as a prompt to deposit, increase stake, or extend a session.

## Welfare-first experience metrics

Positive experience is measured by comprehension, voluntary stopping, limit adherence, absence of loss-chasing, accessibility, fairness confidence, and verified public benefit — not by time-on-device, spins per session, loss volume, deposit frequency, or reactivation after risk.

## Deployment boundary

Real-money deployment stays blocked until the shared system independently passes age/identity, jurisdiction/license, payments/AML, security, RNG/math certification, privacy, responsible-gambling, accessibility, and public-impact audit gates.
