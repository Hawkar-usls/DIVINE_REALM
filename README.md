## Mission

DIVINE_REALM is the **narrative and public-impact surface** of a welfare-first slot architecture. It explores mythic, symbolic, and celestial presentation without allowing that presentation to manipulate gambling intensity.

Its core rule is simple:

> Story, spirituality, charity, community, art, sound, or symbolism must never be used to make wagering, losing, depositing, or continuing play feel morally required, redemptive, sacred, or socially necessary.

## Shared architecture

```text
FAIRNESS / RNG / GAME MATH
          ↓
WELFARE & ELIGIBILITY GATE
          ↓
        SSlot
  reference game client
          ↓
     DIVINE_REALM
 narrative + impact surface
          ↓
 PUBLIC IMPACT LEDGER
          ↓
    INDEPENDENT AUDIT
```

`SSlot` owns the reference mathematics/game surface. DIVINE_REALM may change art, story, sound, accessibility-friendly atmosphere, and privacy-preserving public-impact visualization. It may **not** alter RTP, RNG, limits, eligibility, risk responses, accounting, or session truth.

## Narrative safety gates

- no wager-as-sacrifice framing;
- no loss-as-redemption or loss-as-charity framing;
- no RNG-as-prophecy, judgement, destiny, or evidence about the player;
- no spiritual rank, blessing, initiation, or access based on spend/loss volume;
- no engineered near-miss drama or outcome-sensitive suspense;
- no charity pressure to deposit or continue;
- no personalized spiritual persuasion based on losses, risk signals, balance, vulnerability, or time of day;
- no risk-triggered bonus, VIP, deposit prompt, reactivation, or higher-stake prompt.

## Social impact

The Realm can show a transparent, privacy-preserving public ledger of operator-funded impact allocations. Community voting may help choose eligible causes, but **voting power cannot depend on spend**, and charitable preference cannot create any gambling advantage.

Impact totals must never be used as a “keep playing” counter.

## Boundary

Religious, mythological, or metaphysical language is creative framing only. It establishes no scientific, theological, medical, prophetic, or supernatural authority. Real-money deployment remains blocked until independent legal, security, AML, fairness/RNG, privacy, accessibility, responsible-gambling, and narrative-coercion review gates pass.
